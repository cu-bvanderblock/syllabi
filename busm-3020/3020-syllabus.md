## About BUSM 3020

**Course Focus:** the use of quantitative tools to interpret and solve important
problems in business and finance. Makes extensive use of spreadsheet modeling,
analysis, and case studies to present material. Targeted at students with an
interest in quantitative methods and modeling.

**Prerequisites:** BUSM 2010, 2011, 2020, 2021 (minimum grade D-). Requires
corequisite of BUSM 3021. Restricted to students pursuing Business Minor (BUSM-MIN).

## Our Learning Goals

After successfully completing this course, you will:

1. Be able to describe and/or explain: an approach to business decision making
   with financial and statistical analysis, regression and its uses in business
   decisions, hypothesis testing, time-value of money, and confidence intervals
   (for means and proportions).
2. Given appropriate information, be able to: draw appropriate conclusions and
   make business decisions using regression analysis, develop confidence
   intervals, select the appropriate sample size, test business hypotheses, and
   solve annuity and unequal cash flow calculations using time value of money
   concepts.

## Assignments and Grading

In this course, your grade consists of **participation (10%)**, homework
**assignments (20%)**, two exams (**25% for the midterm** and **25% for the
final**), and a **team project (20%)**.

All graded items and final course grades are considered final one week after
being made available to students, unless otherwise stated. Please **check Canvas
for posted grades**.

**Final course grades may be curved up, but never down**, in accordance with the
Leeds Grading Guidelines.

### Participation & Attendance (10%)

Working in Business Analytics is a practice in applying theoretical concepts and
adopting a "Learn by Doing" approach. To reinforce this, a portion of your grade
will be based on in-class participation. **For each class, 1 participation point
is awarded for attendance.**

### Homework and PlayPosit Videos (20%)

After each of our lectures, you are assigned corresponding homework **assignments
in McGraw-Hill Connect**. The due dates for these modules are indicated on the
course calendar as well as on Connect. Generally, each assignment is due by
midnight the Sunday following our lecture. Submissions will not be allowed once
the deadline has passed.

In addition to in-class clicker questions, you will have the opportunity to
**earn additional points by completing online videos delivered via PlayPosit in
Canvas**. There will be several for-credit questions embedded throughout the
videos. You will only have one chance to answer these questions, unlike with your
homework assignments. Please check the course schedule for the due dates.

### Team Project (20%)

In a professional setting, business analytics is almost always a collaborative
exercise with peers and stakeholders in your organization. You'll be given one
opportunity to apply analytic concepts to real world business problems in a team
setting, approximately three students to a team. Students are expected to share
the workload on this case assignment and will have a chance for a peer assessment.

Each team will also deliver a short **in-class presentation** of their findings.
The presentation is part of the team project grade and gives you practice
communicating analytic results to an audience.

### Exams (25% each: midterm and final)

There will be two open-book, open-note exams administered through Canvas. They
will be a combination of true/false, case study, and multiple choice questions;
and will require the use of Excel. The exam dates are listed on the course
schedule, and the final exam schedule is posted on the CU website. Discuss any
conflicts with the exam dates by the end of the first week of class.

The **final is partially cumulative**: it centers on material covered after the
midterm but also revisits key concepts from the first half of the course.

Practice questions are provided in advance.

## Use of AI

CU Boulder provides **ChatGPT Edu** to students (access begins Aug 14, 2026,
after you complete the required Student Training Course in Canvas). For this
course:

- **BUSM 3020 is theory- and statistics-heavy**: the emphasis is on knowledge
  you must *learn and retain*, not merely apply. Treat AI as a **learning
  assistant only**. (Its corequisite, BUSM 3021, integrates AI more directly
  into the coursework.)
- **Exams: not permitted.** Using any AI tool during exams or quizzes is
  prohibited and treated as an Honor Code violation.
- **Study aid: permitted.** You may use AI to explain concepts, check your
  reasoning, or generate practice problems.
- **Homework: permitted, but use it to learn.** AI assistance on homework is
  allowed; however, using it to *produce answers* instead of understanding the
  material works to your detriment; exams reward what you have genuinely learned.
